Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (sample document for evaluation)

1. Working arrangements
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Team leads set the on-site days for each team. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

2. Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is tracked separately and does not reduce annual leave.

3. Expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims above 200 euros need written approval from a manager before the purchase.

4. IT support
IT support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 IST. Urgent outages outside these hours are reported through the on-call phone line listed on the intranet.

5. Security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 180 days. Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts.

6. Onboarding
Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 90 days.
